CROSS-VENUE FAIR VALUE

One market can have multiple realities.

Price discovery moves between venues. Metanyx can combine Monaco execution state with Binance and Hyperliquid reference data while accounting for venue basis, latency and feed health.

01 / NORMALIZE

Comparable market state

Venue-specific books, trades and timestamps are normalized into a common event layer before signals consume them.

02 / WEIGH

Reference confidence

Freshness and agreement matter. Stale or conflicting references can be discounted rather than allowed to drive a quote.

03 / ADAPT

Venue-aware basis

A persistent difference between venues is not automatically an opportunity; learned basis helps distinguish structure from dislocation.

ADAPTIVE QUOTING

Quotes should respond to pressure, not merely follow price.

Short-horizon signals can inform when to join, cross, widen, skew or wait. The objective is measured execution, not activity for its own sake.

01 / MICROSTRUCTURE

Read the book

Microprice, spread, depth and order-flow imbalance describe pressure that a midpoint alone cannot express.

02 / TIMING

Model arrivals

Trade arrivals, predicted drift and toxicity estimates can change how aggressively a strategy refreshes its orders.

03 / INVENTORY

Price the position

Inventory and funding can shift bid and ask behavior so quoting decisions reflect the risk already held.

EXECUTION + RISK

Fail closed when the market view is uncertain.

Market making compounds small decisions quickly. Controls therefore sit inside the strategy and execution loop rather than only in an after-the-fact dashboard.

01 / OWNERSHIP

Strategy-owned orders

Order ownership helps isolate runs and prevents one strategy from silently managing another strategy’s exposure.

02 / LIMITS

Explicit boundaries

Position limits, live-trading gates and emergency controls constrain what an automated strategy is allowed to do.

03 / RECOVERY

Reconcile reality

Reconnect logic checks venue orders and positions before quoting resumes, reducing risk from stale local assumptions.
