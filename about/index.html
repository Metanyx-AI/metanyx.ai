OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Fast does not have to mean opaque.

Metanyx is designed around three principles that shape the data, strategy and execution layers.

01 / SELF-CUSTODY

Ownership stays anchored

User-controlled wallets and explicit venue permissions remain the trust anchor for trading activity.

02 / OBSERVABILITY

Decisions leave evidence

Events, signals, latency, orders and confirmed fills are designed to be inspected, replayed and audited.

03 / FAIL CLOSED

Uncertainty reduces risk

Stale feeds, reference disagreement and unresolved exchange state should pause or reduce exposure rather than be ignored.
